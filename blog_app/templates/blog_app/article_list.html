About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Isfahan, with academic interests at the intersection of artificial intelligence, algorithms, and mathematical logic.

My interests include intelligent and multi-agent systems, preference and opponent modelling, artificial intelligence, and trustworthy & sustainable intelligent systems. I am particularly interested in problems that combine theoretical reasoning with implementation and experimental evaluation.

Alongside my coursework, I work on technical and research-oriented projects in computer science and maintain this website as a place for technical work, notes, reports, and occasional writing.
